title: Notice on the phased removal of the CentOS mirror
name: removeal_of_centos_legacy_240624

date: 2024-06-24
---

As CentOS 7 reaches end of life on June 30, all traditional CentOS releases will no longer be supported, and upstream will soon clear the contents of its mirror repositories. To minimize disruption caused by the retirement of the CentOS mirror, we have decided to remove it in phases.

**2024-06-24:** Stop synchronizing this repository with upstream

**2024-06-30:** End off-campus access to this repository

**2026-06-30:** Remove its files and end on-campus access to this repository

For on-campus users:

Please note that ELRepo and EPEL will also soon end support for CentOS 7, and CentOS 7 will no longer receive security updates. You should migrate to another distribution as soon as possible. We currently do not provide mirrors of ELRepo Archive or EPEL Archive.

For off-campus users:

After June 30, 2024, we will no longer provide the CentOS repository. Please migrate to a `centos-vault` mirror operated by another mirror site.

Thank you for your understanding and support.
