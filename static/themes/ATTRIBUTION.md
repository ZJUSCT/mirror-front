# Theme assets

These optional themes include third-party artwork associated with Terraria
(Re-Logic) and Stardew Valley (ConcernedApe). All rights in this artwork remain
with the respective rights holders. This project claims no ownership of the
artwork and grants no license to it; the repository's MIT license does not
cover third-party game artwork. Inclusion does not imply endorsement by the
game creators.

The `*-still.png` images are first-frame derivatives of animated artwork for
visitors who prefer reduced motion. No game audio is included.

Fonts are self-hosted:

- Fusion Pixel: license in `shared/fusion-pixel-OFL.txt`.
- Silkscreen: Google Fonts, `ofl/silkscreen/Silkscreen-Regular.ttf`;
  license in `shared/silkscreen-OFL.txt`.
- VT323: Google Fonts, `ofl/vt323/VT323-Regular.ttf`;
  license in `shared/vt323-OFL.txt`.

Google Fonts source: https://github.com/google/fonts
