<div className="faq-list">
  <details open>
    <summary>
      What advantages does ZJU Mirror offer over other public mirror sites?
    </summary>
    <div className="faq-answer">
      <p>
        The servers are located inside the ZJU campus network, so laboratories
        with slow or restricted public-internet access can still reach the
        mirrors reliably. We also prioritize sustained teaching and research
        needs on campus, including less widely used repositories when there is
        a genuine need.
      </p>
    </div>
  </details>

<details>
  <summary>What kinds of mirror services are available?</summary>
  <div className="faq-answer">
    <p>
      Most distributions and repositories are provided as full HTTP mirrors,
      including Ubuntu, Debian, Arch Linux, CTAN, and ROS. Some services, such
      as PyPI and Anaconda, use caching proxies. Git repositories are published
      using their project-specific protocols and layouts.
    </p>
  </div>
</details>

  <details>
    <summary>How can I tell whether a mirror is up to date?</summary>
    <div className="faq-answer">
      <p>
        The homepage and file listings show each mirror’s current
        synchronization state and most recent update time. For additional
        observations, visit ZJU’s status page in the CERNET mirror federation.
      </p>
      <p>
        <a href="https://mirrors.cernet.edu.cn/site/ZJU">
          View detailed synchronization status
        </a>
      </p>
    </div>
  </details>
</div>
